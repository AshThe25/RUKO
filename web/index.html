The blind spot

Nobody watches the room

A scammer doesn't break your encryption. They call, say your account freezes in ten minutes, and let you do the rest. Every technical signal stays clean — because you sent the money.

Authenticated

Biometrics, OTP, device fingerprint — all green, because the victim really is the account holder.

Unwatched

The signal lives in the conversation. Authority, threats, deadlines, secrecy — minutes before the transfer.

Irreversible

Once the PIN is entered the money is gone. Protection has to arrive before the confirm button, not after.

How it works

Six steps, all on the phone

None of them need signal. None of them reach a server.

01

It hears the room, and forgets it

Microphone live during a protected session, notification-visible. Audio is processed in memory and discarded — never stored, never uploaded, never leaving the native layer.

02

Speech becomes text, on the phone

An on-device recogniser turns a rolling window into words. Nothing about the conversation reaches a server — there is no server in this path.

03

A small model scores six pressure tactics

Not "is this a scam" — too big a question. Instead: authority, threat, urgency, payment instruction, secrecy, credential request. Six numbers, plus how confident it actually is.

04

It checks boring local facts

No AI. Paid this person before? What do you normally send? On a call with an unknown number? All answered from your own phone's history.

05

Arithmetic decides — not the AI

Every signal × a fixed weight × a trust gate, added up. A pure function: same evidence, same score, every phone, every time.

06

You get the reasons, not a verdict

Don't pay is the easy button. Continuing takes one deliberate extra tap — because sometimes Ruko will be wrong, and you're the one who knows.

Honest limits

What it can't do

Every safety product that overclaims gets caught eventually. So here are the limits, first, in our own words.

—

It cannot record your phone call

Android reserves call audio for privileged system apps. Ruko hears the room through the ordinary microphone: works on speaker, deaf at the ear, and says so.

—

It cannot cancel a payment inside GPay or PhonePe

No public API lets one app block another's transaction. Ruko reads what's visible on screen and interrupts the decision, not the transfer. The real fix is OEM integration.

—

It doesn't understand every language yet

English and romanised Hinglish today. Devanagari needs a tokeniser roughly five times larger — a real gap, not a rounding error.

—

It will sometimes be wrong

0.657 macro F1 on hand-written text it has never seen — useful, not solved. That is exactly why arithmetic makes the final call, and "continue anyway" is always one tap away.

Beyond one payment

Small amounts add up

A cap on a single payment never sees six ₹500 purchases in an afternoon. Ruko watches the window, not just the transaction.

Cumulative spend

Six ₹500 payments reach ₹3,000 without one of them being large. The trusted contact sets the line — ₹500 by default, and only they can change it — and is told when the total crosses it, not for every purchase, which is how families end up muting the alerts.

Approval, not just a notice

Past the limit, Ruko holds the screen until a trusted contact approves or declines in their own app. Holds lapse on their own after thirty minutes: a guardian who is asleep must not leave someone stranded.

Grooming and sextortion

The same six pressure signals appear when a child is being groomed — secrecy, threat, isolation. Ruko reads them with on-device rules, and safeguarding advice is deliberately excluded so warning a child never looks like abusing one.

The other app

Something to test it against

Ruko is only interesting if it works on a payment app it was not built for. So we wrote a second, separate one — PayNow, a working UPI wallet — and gave it no knowledge of Ruko at all.

No shared code

PayNow and Ruko share no module, no broadcast, no private channel. PayNow draws a payment screen and posts ordinary message notifications, exactly as any wallet does. Ruko has to read it through the accessibility tree and the notification listener, the same way it would read PhonePe. A demo where the two halves are wired together proves nothing.

Three situations

A bank-impersonation thread that ends in a ₹500 “KYC verification” transfer. A grooming conversation that ends in a ₹2,000 demand. And a child topping up a game, where no message is threatening and no single payment is large — the one that language alone cannot catch.

An unlimited balance

The wallet never runs out. That is a demo decision with a point to it: if a payment gets stopped, it was stopped by Ruko, never by PayNow running short of imaginary money.

Installing takes four steps.

This is a sideloaded APK rather than a Play Store listing, because Ruko needs the microphone and accessibility permissions that Play policy restricts — for good reasons. You are trusting us with those, so everything it does with them stays on the phone.

  1. 1Settings → Security → allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  2. 2Open ruko.apk and confirm. Then paynow.apk, the demo wallet.
  3. 3Grant Ruko accessibility access and “display over other apps”. These two are what let it read a payment screen and interrupt one — without them it runs, but it cannot come between you and a payment.
  4. 4Microphone and notification access are what add calls and messages. Ruko works with less, it just sees less.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer, 64-bit. Debug-signed prototype: uninstall any earlier copy first.
